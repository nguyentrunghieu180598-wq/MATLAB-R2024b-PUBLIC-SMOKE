## Why one new candidate is discriminating
Source exact first-order TF: `tau*y'=u-y`, `tau=.001s`, source ideal `.25<=u<=.75` for bounded real inputs, no independent physical reset. For a real solution from actual `y0`: `y0+(.25-y0)(1-exp(-t/tau)) <= y(t) <= y0+(.75-y0)(1-exp(-t/tau))`. No requirement to know exact event times to establish this **necessary bound**, though a logged point must be a meaningful numerical/physical output to be used as evidence of failure.

`ode23t` trapezoidal nominal damping coefficient under a hypothetical fixed step `h=.05s`: `R=(1-h/(2*tau))/(1+h/(2*tau))=-12/13`. This explains *possible* numerical ringing after an excitation, but does NOT certify actual h at event or identify the first state perturbation. At `h=.005`, pure trapezoid still has `R=-3/7`; **smaller MaxStep alone is no proof of repair**. The proposed `ode23tb` TR-BDF2 is a distinct stiff numerical method, not previously qualified or assumed to be stable for this complete model. The source code/model/TF/controller is unchanged.
